# Templates

This directory holds controlled legislative engineering templates for Legislative Manager.

## Purpose

Templates provide reusable structure for package placement and jurisdiction-organized engineering surfaces (Federal, State, County, City, School). They are engineering aids that support consistent package construction under applicable Kansas Legislative Engineering Library (KLS) standards.

## Authority and Limits

- Templates must conform to applicable KLS standards.
- Templates are engineering aids, not enacted law.
- Use of a template does not authorize legislation.
- AI may not invent authority through templates.
- Human Engineer acceptance remains required where applicable.

## Current Status

Template population is reserved for subsequent controlled work. No operative legislative templates are present in this directory at this time.
