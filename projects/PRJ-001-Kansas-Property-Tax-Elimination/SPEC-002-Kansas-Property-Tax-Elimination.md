## 1. Purpose

This specification engineers **PRJ-001 — Kansas Property Tax Elimination** as the first real Kansas legislative package under the Constitutional Engineering Office.

It defines objectives, scope, stakeholders, impacts, required artifacts, control affinities, review gates, risks, assumptions, and the project roadmap.

**This specification does not draft legislation.**  
**This specification does not draft constitutional language.**  
**This specification does not produce fiscal estimates.**  
**This specification does not publish.**

---

## 2. Objectives

### 2.1 Primary Engineering Objective

Establish a complete, lifecycle-controlled engineering package family that can later support Human Engineer-authorized drafting of Kansas instruments to eliminate Kansas property taxation as a standing revenue structure, subject to constitutional supremacy, statutory integrity, and AGCL/NBBF/CDT reference boundaries.

### 2.2 Supporting Objectives

1. Identify all constitutional and statutory impact surfaces before any operative text is drafted  
2. Separate constitutional-layer work (`CAP`) from statutory-layer work (`BILL` / `AMD`)  
3. Engineer fiscal-note structure without inventing estimates  
4. Engineer definitions before drafting uses those terms  
5. Assemble a publication package structure without publishing  
6. Preserve ownership boundaries for AGCL, NBBF, and CDT  
7. Produce a roadmap from Pre-Draft engineering through future Authorized Drafting and Publication gates  
8. Validate that KLS-001 through KLS-006 are sufficient for a real Kansas package family  

### 2.3 Non-Objectives

1. Determine final Kansas political outcomes  
2. Select a replacement tax design in operative legal text  
3. Quantify revenue, expenditure, or distribution effects in this phase  
4. Ratify, enact, or publish anything under this CWC
